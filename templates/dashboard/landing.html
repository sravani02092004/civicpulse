How CivicPulse Works

A simple flow connecting citizens, AI, NGOs, and past experience.

1

Report

Citizen submits a civic issue.

2

Understand

AI categorizes the issue.

3

Assign

Issue is connected with an NGO.

4

Recall

Past similar experiences are retrieved.

5

Resolve

NGO takes action and records the result.

CivicRecall Agent

CivicPulse doesn't treat every complaint as a completely new problem. Hindsight gives the platform persistent organizational memory of previous civic issue experiences.

RETAIN

Resolved civic issues and their outcomes are stored as organizational experience.

RECALL

Similar previous issues can be retrieved when a new complaint arrives.

REFLECT

Previous experiences are analyzed to provide practical recommendations for the current issue.
